The Whole You, privacy notice

The Whole You is a free 3D dream adventure game made by Advik and published as a ZeroOrigine adopted project.

  • No accounts. You never type an email or a password.
  • No ads and no in-game purchases.
  • Your progress (characters, coins, chapters, the name you type) is saved only in your own browser on your own device. We never see it.
  • The only thing the game sends to us is one anonymous count each time the page is opened, so we know how many times it was played. No cookie, no device id, no name.
  • The 3D engine (three.js) and the font load from public content networks (jsdelivr, Google Fonts). Those services see your IP address the way any website does.

Questions: hello@zeroorigine.com
